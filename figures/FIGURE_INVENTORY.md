## Explicitly NOT included, and why

- **No 16-blade rotor render.** The full-rotor circular pattern was never successfully generated
  (SolidWorks installation-level API limitation, item O8) — showing one would misrepresent CAD
  work that does not exist. Figure 5's plan-view schematic instead shows the single verified
  22.5° sector, correctly labeled as "1 of 16 blades."
- **No mesh image.** No volume mesh exists (item blocked, see `03_cfd/CFD_STATUS.md`).
- **No pressure/velocity/Mach contour.** No CFD solution exists — there is nothing to contour.
- **No performance plot** (thrust, torque, power, efficiency vs. anything). These are `[CFD-TBD]`
  in the design output and have not been produced by any solver.
