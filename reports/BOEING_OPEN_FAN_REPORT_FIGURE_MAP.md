## Notes

- **Figures 4 and 6** (workflow-status and project-maturity graphics) were built for the README
  and top-level project documentation, not for this technical report — the report's own prose
  (§1, §3, §8) and tables (§5.4, §6.5, §7.4) already carry that information in report form. They
  are listed here for completeness of the figure inventory, not because they are embedded in
  `BOEING_OPEN_FAN_FINAL_REPORT.md`.
- **No figure in this report depicts a CFD result** (mesh, contour, or performance plot) — none
  exist. Figure 5 is explicitly labeled a schematic of verified *geometry and topology only*.
- **No figure depicts a 16-blade rotor.** Figure 5's plan-view panel shows the single verified
  22.5° sector, labeled "1 of 16 blades," reflecting that the full-rotor pattern was never
  successfully generated (a documented SolidWorks limitation, not a CFD requirement).
- Full per-figure provenance, including source scripts and what was explicitly excluded, is in
  `figures/FIGURE_INVENTORY.md` — this map is the report-specific subset of that inventory.
